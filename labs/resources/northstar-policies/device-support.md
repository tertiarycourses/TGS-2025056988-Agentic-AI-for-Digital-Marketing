# NS-POL-DEVICE-2026-04 - Device Support Policy

Effective: 1 April 2026
Access scope: learner

Employees requesting a replacement laptop must provide the asset tag, a concise fault description and either a diagnostics screenshot or the service-desk diagnostic reference. If the device cannot start, the employee may state that no screenshot is available and provide the last successful startup date.

The service desk may prepare a replacement draft after identity and asset ownership are confirmed. A person must review any request marked High or Critical before it is submitted to operations.

For a P2 request, the acknowledgement target is four business hours. This target is not a promise of resolution.
