# NS-POL-PRIORITY-2026-06 - Request Priority Guide

Effective: 15 June 2026
Access scope: learner

Priority is based on impact and urgency. Critical is reserved for widespread service loss or a severe safety or security impact. High applies when a core job function is blocked for one or more users and no reasonable workaround exists. Normal applies to limited impact with a workaround. Low applies to information or planned change requests.

The P2 acknowledgement target is three business hours from validated intake. A service owner reviews exceptions and entitlement-related changes.
